ATS Optimization & Resume Best Practices

This AI Resume Builder is engineered following modern Applicant Tracking System (ATS) parsing standards:

  • Clean Hierarchy: Single-column document structure easily indexed by Workday, Lever, Greenhouse, and Taleo.
  • Action Verbs & Impact: AI transforms passive descriptions into quantitative achievements (e.g. "Spearheaded...", "Accelerated by 40%...").
  • True-to-Fact Rules: The AI never invents fake metrics or technologies; it elevates your genuine experience into professional phrasing.
  • Vector PDF: Clean PDF export preserves searchable text characters for ATS scanners.
